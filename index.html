ERP Data Quality Audit & Demand Forecasting Model

Deliverables for an industrial equipment manufacturer, from an unreliable ERP to forecast-driven reorder points.

Five deliverables trace the work end to end: an audit that finds and remediates every type of data quality error in the ERP, a demand model that forecasts each item's usage over its supplier lead time, that forecast delivered as reorder points and order quantities inside the ERP's purchasing screen, and the MLOps monitoring that keeps it honest. Full detail is in the README.
